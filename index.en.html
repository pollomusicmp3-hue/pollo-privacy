Privacy Policy — POLLO Music Player

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. Plain-language summary

POLLO is an offline, on-device music player. We have no server, no account, no tracking. Everything you play, import or adjust is saved only on your own device. The only external connection is optional and turned off by default: if you enable "Download lyrics automatically" (or tap "Find lyrics"), the app queries the public service lrclib.net, sending only the song's title/artist/album/duration to fetch the lyrics. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

2. What data does POLLO access?

2.1 Local music files

When you import a folder or files via the "Import" button, POLLO reads the audio files (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA) directly from your device's storage. The tags (title, artist, album, cover art) are extracted locally. POLLO only requests read permission — it cannot alter, move or delete your original files.

  • Where it is saved: inside the app's private storage (/data/data/com.sonora.player/), accessible only by POLLO.
  • Where it goes: nowhere. The files are not sent to the internet.

2.2 Playback history

POLLO records locally which songs you played, for how long, and when. This feeds the Recap, the achievements and the recommendations.

  • Where it is saved: the app's localStorage (also private).
  • Where it goes: nowhere. You can clear everything in the Dev Panel (tap the logo 7× → "FULL RESET").

2.3 Preferences and settings

Themes, favorites, playlists, equalizer, etc. are saved in localStorage.

2.4 Lyrics lookup (optional · uses the internet · off by default)

If you enable "Download lyrics automatically" in Settings (or tap "Find lyrics" on the lyrics screen), POLLO queries the free, public service lrclib.net, sending only the song's title, artist, album and duration to locate the lyrics. We do not send your identity, account, IP-tracking or history. The received lyrics are stored locally (cache) so they work offline afterwards. With the option turned off (the default), the app makes no network connection at all.

4. Does POLLO COLLECT any personal data?

No. There is no analytics, telemetry, automatic crash reporting, login, sign-up, unique identifier, IP tracking, fingerprinting, or sharing with third parties. The app does not embed any analytics or tracking SDK — no Google/Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer or Facebook SDK. It also does not use the advertising ID (AAID): the permission is removed from the app. Error diagnostics stay on the device only (you copy them manually if you want to report a bug).

5. Does POLLO SHARE data with third parties?

The only native services used are Android's own (Storage Access Framework, MediaSession, Local Notifications scheduled in the OS) and Google Play Billing for the optional one-time purchase (Play processes the payment; no purchase data goes to us or to third parties). The only possible external connection is optional and off by default: if you enable lyrics lookup, a query to lrclib.net (a free, public lyrics database) sending only the song's title/artist/album/duration. In this case we do not send personal identifiers, account or tracking. It can be turned off in Settings.

6. One-time purchase (optional)

POLLO offers an optional one-time purchase (no subscription) for premium features, via Google Play Billing. The payment is processed entirely by Google Play; POLLO neither receives nor stores your card data, and no purchase information is sent to the developer or to third parties.

7. Children

POLLO does not collect data, so there is no special handling for minors. We recommend supervision for any app, but POLLO is safe in this respect — no accounts, no fields for personal info, no advertising.

8. Easter eggs, achievements and seasonals

Easter eggs and achievements track actions within the app itself; the counters stay on the device only. On special dates, POLLO activates a temporary theme and may schedule a local notification for 00:00 of the day via Android's LocalNotifications — the scheduling is done only on the device, with no network.

9. Backup and removal

  • Backup: Settings/Dev Panel → "Export (copies JSON)" → paste it wherever you like. The backup includes no personal data or logs.
  • Removal: uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

10. Changes to this policy

Any change will be noted in the "Last updated" line at the top. Material changes will carry an explicit notice before the feature is enabled.
